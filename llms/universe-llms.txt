# Universe

> Universe is a no-code website builder that lets anyone design and publish a custom website from a phone, tablet, or computer using a drag-and-drop GRID block system, with built-in commerce, mail, analytics, and SEO tools. Winner of a 2023 Apple Design Award, with more than one million sites built. Backed by GV and General Catalyst (YC W18). Universe publishes no public developer API.

## Product

- [Universe](https://univer.se/): Homepage for the Universe website builder
- [Web app](https://web.univer.se/): Get started building a site in the browser
- [iOS app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/universe-website-builder/id1211437633): Universe Website Builder on the App Store
- [Pricing](https://univer.se/pricing): Free, Creator, Pro, and Max plans
- [Templates](https://univer.se/templates): Professionally designed site templates

## Notes for agents
